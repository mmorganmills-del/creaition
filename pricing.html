Changes, made simple.

Your first revision is free on every site and covers up to 5 items. After that, we handle changes case by case, based on what you ask for and what's been delivered.

What counts as a revision:

  • A text section
  • An image
  • An interactivity change, including photo changes for interactive elements
  • A product change to wording, specs or details

Extra revisions: $10.00 each. Without a plan, buy them as you need them. On Starter or Creator, add up to 3 a month. Infinity includes up to 5 a day. Need more than that? Request More

FAQ

Do you take a deposit?

No. Every project is paid in full upfront, and that starts the build.

What's the difference between a simple page and a feature page?

A simple page uses your site's existing style, like About, Team or FAQ ($25). A feature page does something: video playback, forms, posting or galleries ($50).

How fast is it?

Home page (landing page) in 72 hours, guaranteed. A full site in 2 weeks; we aim for 7–10 business days. Turnaround starts Pending: when the turnaround clock starts.

Do I own my site?

Yes, always. On a plan, we monitor and manage it for you.

Who launches my site?

Without a plan, you launch it on your own hosting or platform. On a plan, we host it for you.

Is my first revision free?

Yes. Your first revision on every site is free and covers up to 5 items.

I need more than 5 pages.

Great. Request More and tell us why. We'll size it with you.
