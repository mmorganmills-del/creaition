FAQ

Do I need a plan?

No. Every build is yours to launch on your own hosting or platform. A plan adds monthly revisions, interactivity updates and ongoing care. Hosting is a separate host-size choice.

Can I change plans?

Plan upgrade/downgrade policy

What does "within reason" mean on Infinity?

Up to 5 revisions a day, so every change gets real care.

When do I pay a host fee?

Only if your site needs hosting or storage, such as customer accounts. You pick the host size; it isn't tied to your plan.
