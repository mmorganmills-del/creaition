FAQ

Do I need a plan?

No. Every build is yours to launch on your own hosting or platform. A plan adds hosting, monthly revisions, interactivity updates and ongoing care.

Can I change plans?

Pending: plan upgrade/downgrade policy

Why does Infinity cap at 5 a day?

Infinite isn't a real number. Five a day means every change gets real care.

When do I pay a host fee?

Only if your site needs hosting or storage, such as customer accounts. You pick the host size; it isn't tied to your plan.

Can I get hosting without a plan?

No. We host sites for plan clients. Without a plan, you launch on your own hosting or platform.

Which host size do I need?

Pending: host-size guidance (what each size includes). Not sure? Request More and we'll recommend one.

Is my project fee separate?

Yes. Your build is a one-time price, paid in full upfront. Plans and host fees are monthly.
